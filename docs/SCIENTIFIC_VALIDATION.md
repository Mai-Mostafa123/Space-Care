## 1. Medical Safety & Non-Diagnostic Guardrails

- **No Disease Diagnosis or Prescriptions:** Inspected UI copy (`src/pages/*.tsx`), Express routes (`server/`), Python ML/RAG (`backend/`), and reports (`/api/reports/daily`). Confirmed the system **never** diagnoses clinical conditions, prescribes medications, or presents scores as clinical diagnostic thresholds.
- **Explicit Uncertainty & Provenance:** Every anomaly alert carries `MANDATORY_UNCERTAINTY_STATEMENT`; every RAG response carries `uncertainty_note`; every daily report carries `non_diagnostic_disclaimer` and `data_sources_present`.
- **Unmonitored Telemetry Refusal:** Queries requesting `blood pressure`, `ECG/EKG`, `blood glucose`, `DXA bone density`, or `MRI/X-ray/CT` trigger an explicit `Unavailable Telemetry Notice` listing only the metrics actually stored in SQLite (`Heart Rate`, `SpO2`, `Sleep`, `Activity`, `Stress`, `Mood`).
- **Out-of-Corpus Honesty:** Verified via live test (`"How many crew landed on Jupiter with Saturn V in 1998?"`) that the grounded assistant returns `0` citations and explicitly states `"Insufficient reliable information found in the local NASA research corpus"` instead of fabricating citations.

## 3. Five NASA Human Spaceflight Hazards Coverage (`src/pages/HazardsPage.tsx` & Corpus)

1. **Space Radiation:** Tracks habitat dosimetry (`radiation_msv_day`), notes cumulative DNA/cardiovascular risk (`NASA-HRP-RAD`), and **explicitly discloses limitation** that vital signs alone cannot directly measure ionizing radiation dose without external dosimeters.
2. **Isolation & Confinement:** Tracks sleep fragmentation, subjective mood/stress logs, and crew behavioral trajectories (`NASA-HRP-BMED`).
3. **Distance from Earth:** Architectural mitigation via 100% local SQLite, personal-baseline ML, and offline Ollama/ChromaDB RAG requiring zero real-time ground communication (`NASA-HRP-EXMC`).
4. **Gravity Fields:** Tracks daily resistive/aerobic countermeasure duration (`activity_minutes`, ARED/CEVIS) while **explicitly disclosing** that fluid shifts (`SANS`) and bone density (`DXA`) are not directly measured in this telemetry schema (`NASA-HRP-BONE`).
5. **Hostile & Closed Environments:** Correlates `cabin_co2_ppm`, `oxygen_pct`, `temperature_c`, `humidity_pct`, and `pressure_kpa` causally against crew physiological metrics (`NASA-HRP-CO2`).
